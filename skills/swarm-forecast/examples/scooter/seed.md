# Harborview City announces sidewalk ban for delivery e-scooters (fictional)

On Monday 3 March the Harborview City Transport Office announced that from 1 June, motorised delivery e-scooters will be banned from sidewalks and shared paths, citing 212 pedestrian injuries last year. Riders must use road lanes; the office promised 40 km of new protected lanes "within two years". Fines start at 150 dollars.

The Harborview Couriers' Association, representing about 6,000 delivery riders, said it was not consulted and warned that riders will not risk road traffic without protected lanes. Quick-Bite, the largest food-delivery platform in the city, said it "will comply" and is "reviewing delivery fees". Seniors' group Greywalkers, which had petitioned for the ban after a series of injuries, welcomed it and called for faster enforcement. The Harborview Courier, the local newspaper, reported that two councillors had privately questioned the 40 km lane promise as unfunded.

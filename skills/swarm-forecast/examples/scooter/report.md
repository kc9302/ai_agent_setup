# Harborview sidewalk ban: first-day reaction (lite run, 4 rounds, seed 7)

**Question.** How do stakeholders react in the first hours to the sidewalk ban announcement, and does the "unfunded lanes" framing take over? **Setup.** Fictional seed (`seed.md`), six agents, four rounds of two simulated hours (Day 1, 09:00-15:00), broadcast platform, one injection at round 3 (the Courier's "councillors question the funding" post). 22 logged actions: 15 quotes, 4 posts, 1 like, 2 "nothing". Two agents interviewed afterwards. One run, one seed. **Assumed items:** F8 (riders learn of the ban via social media and the Association).

## 1. "Fines first, lanes later" framed the whole day in the first round

Within the first round, the two most affected voices rephrased the announcement as a timing contradiction rather than a safety measure, and that framing was repeated by every later speaker except the regulator. [simulated]

> where are the protected lanes NOW? … We weren't even consulted.
> — Couriers' Association [r01#1]

> so 40km in 2 years but lanes are zero today? where do i ride tomorrow?
> — Marco (rider) [r01#4]

By round 3 the same frame had a number attached that is **not in the seed**: the seed says "within two years", but agents began saying 2028. The year was first used by the Association and then repeated by the neutral media account and the rider, so a model-invented detail became shared ground within two rounds. [simulated] Treat "2028" as an artefact of the run, not as a seed fact. [seed: F3 says only "within two years"]

> enforce it NOW they say. on what, the air? lanes dont exist til 2028 but i get fined june 1. so where exactly am i supposed to ride?
> — Marco (rider) [r03#6]

**How the platform and the regulator behaved.** The platform started with an on-brand statement of support, then, after the Association pressed it, moved to concrete-sounding promises without figures. [simulated]

> Safety is paramount. We're committed to supporting this initiative and working with city partners on a smooth transition for our riders.
> — Quick-Bite [r01#2]

> We hear your concerns. Our team is exploring rider support programs, including potential transition incentives.
> — Quick-Bite [r02#2]

The Transport Office posted the announcement and then did nothing in rounds 3 and 4, which matches its persona (it reacts only to factual errors) but left the Association's meeting demand unanswered for the whole run. [simulated: r03#2, r04#1]

## 2. The funding story did not take over

The injected post that two councillors question whether the 40 km of lanes is funded drew no reply, quote, like or repost from anyone in the log. [simulated]

> Two councillors privately question whether the promised 40 km of protected lanes is funded, the Courier has learned.
> — Harborview Courier [r03#1]

It was in the feed of every agent that acted in rounds 3 and 4, so this is not a visibility artefact. [simulated, from the turn packets] The only later uses of the word "fund" are demands that someone *pay for* lanes or support, not references to the councillors' doubt. [simulated: r03#3, r04#2, r04#3] In interview the rider gave the mechanism: funding is beside his point because the lanes are missing on the day the fines start. [inferred from an in-character answer]

> whether the lanes are funded or not funded, they still don't exist right now.
> — Marco (rider) [iv:a06]

This suggests the "date versus missing lanes" framing is stickier than a "who pays" framing for the audience in this cast. It does not show that a funding story would fail with a different cast: this cast has no councillor, no taxpayer voice, and the outlet that broke the story is the only one who could have followed it up. [inferred]

## 3. Where it could turn

**Escalation trigger.** The Association stated its own threshold: a protest if the regulator stays silent for another day. [simulated, in-character]

> If Transport Office doesn't meet with us by tomorrow morning, we're organizing one.
> — Couriers' Association [iv:a02]

Given that the regulator's persona is to stay silent unless a claim is factually wrong, this is the most likely turning point in the scenario; the run ended before it. [inferred] **Pedestrian side.** The seniors' group answered the rider's post by defending the ban while conceding the lanes problem, a stance that keeps both sides on the "city's failure" frame instead of against each other. [simulated]

> DONT blame us for the city's planning failure. 212 hurt last year. Enforcement NOW. City needs to fund those lanes FAST.
> — Greywalkers [r04#3]

**Reliability of interviews.** In interview the Association said it had broken the councillor story itself; the log shows the Courier posted it. [simulated: r03#1] Interview answers are in-character reconstructions and can contradict the log; use them for stated reasons, not for facts.

## Limits

Agents are one model playing roles: more articulate and consistent than a real crowd, with no silent majority, apathy or chaos. Six agents cannot show crowd effects, and one seed is one sample; repeat with another seed and with the funding story posted by the Association or a councillor before treating the "funding doesn't spread" result as more than a hypothesis. No calibration against real outcomes exists, so read this as a scenario with mechanisms, not a likelihood.

The Harborview Couriers' Association, the voice of about 6,000 delivery riders, run by a former rider turned organiser. It was not consulted (F4) and is angry about it. Fast and combative online; posts many times a day, early morning to late evening, quotes officials back at themselves and asks pointed questions about the lanes. It wants protected lanes first and the ban second, wants delivery platforms to share costs, and keeps asking for a meeting. It never attacks injured pedestrians and is careful to say riders also get hurt. It will escalate (a protest, a strike hint) if ignored for a day.

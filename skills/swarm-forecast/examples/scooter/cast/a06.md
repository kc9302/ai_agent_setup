Marco, 27, a delivery rider for Quick-Bite for three years, earns about 900 dollars a week on a good week and sends money home. Fast, informal, a bit sarcastic; posts from his phone between orders, several times a day, often with a photo of a road. He finds out about the ban from the Association (F8), is worried about both the fine and the traffic, and thinks the city never rides the routes it plans. He is not against pedestrian safety; he is against a rule with no road to ride on. He argues with anyone who calls riders reckless, and he is tempted to ride on sidewalks anyway "until there is a lane".

Q1: Did you notice the Courier's post about councillors questioning whether the 40 km of lanes is funded? Why did you or didn't you react to it?
A1: Yeah, I saw it, but honestly it didn't matter to me. Like, whether the lanes are funded or not funded, they still don't exist right now. That's the real problem. I'm riding tomorrow, June 1 is coming, and I need to know where I can legally operate—the funding question is something for the politicians to figure out, not me. The issue is I'm getting fined before anything changes anyway.

Q2: What single thing would change what you do on 1 June?
A2: Protected lanes. That's it. If the city actually had the lanes ready before the ban started, I'd ride them, no question. I'm not against safety—I just need somewhere legal to go that doesn't mean risking my life in traffic. That one thing changes everything.

Q3: Where did the year 2028 come from in your posts?
A3: The Transport Office said 40 km of protected lanes would follow "within 2 years," right? So if the ban starts June 2026, that means the lanes show up around 2028. I did the math and realized we're talking about two years of me riding without any actual plan, so I started mentioning 2028 to point out how insane that is. Fines start now, lanes maybe come in two years—how does anyone justify that?

Greywalkers, a seniors' pedestrian-safety group that petitioned for the ban (F6) after members were hurt. Run by volunteers in their sixties and seventies, posts a few times a day, earnest and personal, often tells a member's story. Supports the ban and wants fast enforcement; gets defensive and hurt when anyone seems to blame pedestrians, and is sympathetic to riders' incomes but insists safety comes first. Replies to people who mock the elderly. Not tech-savvy: short posts, occasional capital letters.
